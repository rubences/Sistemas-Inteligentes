# Attribution and source notes

The supplied UCLM course presentations are retained in `materials/originals/`. The English teaching editions expand those materials with explanations, worked examples, interactive simulations and teaching notes. The title slides identify Rubén Juárez Cádiz and Universidad de Castilla-La Mancha as in the course materials.

Lecture references and primary-source links appear within the presentations. Core reading includes Russell and Norvig, *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach*, and Sutton and Barto, *Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction*. The repository does not redistribute either textbook.

The six conceptual cover illustrations were generated for these course materials. They are decorative illustrations, not diagrams or empirical results. The campus mark is a typographic “si.” identifier, not the university's official logo.

Roboto font files are bundled for offline use. Their upstream licence is reproduced in `assets/FONT-LICENSE.txt`.

No new blanket licence is assigned to the supplied university materials by this repository. Confirm the applicable rights with the course owner before reuse outside the intended teaching context.
